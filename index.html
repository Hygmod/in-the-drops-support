In the Drops

Support and privacy information for the offline iPhone bike-racing game.

Privacy

In the Drops does not collect, transmit, sell, or share personal data. The app has no accounts, sign-in, analytics, advertising, tracking, or cloud service. It does not make network requests while you play.

The game stores your chosen difficulty, your sound on/off preference, and whether you have finished a race. These values are kept only in the app's local storage on your device and are never sent to us. Deleting the app removes them.

Graphics and audio are generated on the device. The only links that leave the app are the Support and Privacy links, which open this page in Safari.

Last updated: November 11, 2026

Support

In the Drops is a self-contained offline game. If something does not work as expected, email the developer with your device model, iOS version, and a short description of the problem.

Contact: inthedropssupport@gmail.com

There is no account, subscription, or online service to manage.
